Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Version 1.0

Rasalaya (“we”, “us”) runs a free, public library of the world's recipes. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, write to privacy@rasalaya.org.

1. What we collect, and why

We collect only what each feature needs:

  • Account signup — your email address and a securely stored password, so you can sign in and save favorites. We record your acceptance of our Terms (date, version, and how you accepted) as proof of consent.
  • Favorites — which recipes you save, so we can show them back to you.
  • Newsletter and recipe sharing — your email address, so we can send the recipe you asked for and, if you opt in, our seasonal newsletter (about twice a month). Newsletter signup always requires a separate confirmation email (double opt-in).
  • Feedback and reviews — the name, rating, and comment you submit. Reviews are read by our team before anything appears on the site.
  • Cookies and similar tech — your cookie choices, plus (only with your consent) basic analytics about how the site is used. See section 3.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used for security, fraud prevention, and keeping the site running.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising profiles.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the library: accounts, favorites, recipe sharing, feedback.
  • To send emails you asked for — and only those. Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe link, honored within 10 business days, and includes our mailing address as required by law.
  • To improve the site (only with analytics consent).
  • To keep the site secure and comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies

When you first visit, a banner asks for your cookie choice before any non-essential cookie is set:

  • Necessary — always on. Keeps you signed in and the site secure. The site cannot work without these.
  • Analytics — off unless you accept. Helps us understand which recipes people love.
  • Marketing — off unless you accept. Used to measure our own newsletter and announcements.

You can change your mind anytime via the link in the footer.

4. Who processes your data

A small number of service providers help us run the site — website hosting, our database and account system, and email delivery. They process your data only on our instructions and only to provide their service. We will keep an up-to-date list of processors here.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • See a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data (we keep a minimal suppression record where the law requires, e.g. to honor “do not email me”).
  • Withdraw consent (unsubscribe, change cookie settings, delete your account) — this does not affect anything we already did lawfully.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@rasalaya.org. We respond within 30 days.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data — for the life of your account, plus the period the law requires us to keep records.
  • Consent records (what version of the Terms you accepted, when, and how) — kept as long as needed to prove consent, even after you unsubscribe or delete your account. We never edit these records; a withdrawal is recorded as a new entry.
  • Newsletter — until you unsubscribe; we then keep only your address on a suppression list so we don't email you again by mistake.

7. Children

Rasalaya is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are for ages 13 and up. Our kid-friendly recipes are family recipes meant to be cooked with adult supervision — children should never cook unsupervised. If we learn we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians can contact us at privacy@rasalaya.org.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify you in advance — by email for account holders and with a notice on the site — and the updated version will show a new “last updated” date. If a change is significant, we may ask you to accept the new version the next time you sign in.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@rasalaya.org. You can also reach us via Instagram.
