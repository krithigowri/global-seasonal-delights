Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Version 2026-10-01-v2

Rasalaya (“we”, “us”) runs a free, public library of the world’s recipes. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Who we are. Rasalaya is an independent project operated by its founder as a sole proprietorship doing business as Rasalaya, based in Florida, USA. We plan to form a Florida limited liability company as the project grows. The way to reach us about anything in this policy is our contact form.

Our Terms of Service explain the rules of using the site; this Privacy Policy is part of those Terms.

1. What we collect, and why

We collect only what each feature needs:

  • Account signup — your name, email address and date of birth, so you can sign in and save favorites. We record your acceptance of our Terms (date, version, and how you accepted) as proof of consent. Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • Favorites — which recipes you save, so we can show them back to you.
  • Newsletter and recipe sharing — your email address, so we can send the recipe you asked for and, if you opt in, our newsletter (twice a month). Newsletter signup always requires a separate confirmation email (double opt-in).
  • Feedback and reviews — the name, rating and comment you submit. Reviews are read by our team before anything appears on the site. If your review is published, the display name you choose appears publicly beside it.
  • Cookies and similar tech — your cookie choices, plus (only with your consent) basic analytics about how the site is used. See section 3.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and pages visited, used for security, fraud prevention and keeping the site running. This means ordinary server logs, kept for 90 days.

Demo mode. Until our backend launches, accounts, favorites and preferences are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) — nothing leaves your device, and nothing is sent to us at all. Forms show a demo confirmation instead of sending.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use it for advertising profiles. We do not use your personal information or reviews to train AI models.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the library: accounts, favorites, recipe sharing, feedback.
  • To send emails you asked for — and only those. Creating an account does not subscribe you to our newsletter. Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe link, honored promptly and in any event within 10 business days, and includes our valid postal mailing address as required by law. Unsubscribing from the newsletter does not stop account or security emails (for example, password resets), which are not marketing.
  • To improve the site (only with analytics consent).
  • To keep the site secure and comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies

When you first visit, a banner asks for your cookie choice before any non-essential cookie is set. Reject all is as prominent as Accept all.

  • Necessary — always on. Keeps you signed in and the site secure. The site cannot work without these, including the cookie that remembers your cookie choices.
  • Analytics — off unless you accept. Helps us understand which recipes people love. We use no session replay or heatmaps.
  • Marketing — off unless you accept. Measures only our own newsletter emails (whether they are opened or clicked) so we can improve them — never advertising profiles.

You can change your mind anytime via the Cookie settings page, which also lists every cookie we set, what it does and how long it lasts. We log your consent (timestamp and policy version); consent records are kept as described in section 6.

4. Who processes your data

A small number of service providers help us run the site. They process your data only on our instructions and only to provide their service:

  • Hosting: Netlify (serves the website).
  • Demo mode: no backend providers — your data stays in your browser.
  • When accounts launch: our database/authentication provider and email provider will be named here, with what each does.

We may disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety, and to a successor if Rasalaya’s operations are transferred, subject to this policy. Data is stored in the United States.

5. Your rights

We offer the same rights to everyone, wherever you live:

  • See a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data. If you delete your account, we keep (1) a minimal suppression record (email and unsubscribe status) so we honor your opt-out, and (2) a record of your acceptance of our Terms (date, version, method) for five years after account closure to defend against legal claims. Withdrawals are logged as new entries; we never edit these records. We delete or de-identify these after that period.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (unsubscribe, change cookie settings, delete your account) — this does not affect anything we already did lawfully.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information — we do not sell or share, and we do not knowingly sell or share information of anyone under 16.
  • Registered users under 18 may remove or request removal of content they posted.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent; we may need to verify your identity using information we already hold. To exercise any right, use our contact form. We confirm your request within 10 business days and respond within 30 days; if we need more time (up to 45 additional days where the law allows), we will tell you why. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision message.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data — for the life of your account, plus the period the law requires us to keep records.
  • Consent records (what version of the Terms you accepted, when, and how) — five years after account closure, as described in section 5.
  • Newsletter — until you unsubscribe; we then keep only your address on a suppression list so we don’t email you again by mistake.
  • Server logs — 90 days.
  • Share recipients — when you use Share, we use the recipient’s email address only to send that one message. We do not add recipients to any list or send them marketing, and we delete the address within 7 days. We identify you as the sender.

7. Children

Rasalaya is a general-audience site for adults and families. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Recipes labeled kid-friendly are written for adults to prepare with children — kid-friendly describes flavor, not safety for every child, and children must always cook with adult supervision.

You must be 13 or older to create an account, subscribe to the newsletter, use the Share feature, or submit reviews. We use a neutral date-of-birth check at signup (never a prompt that coaches the answer). If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians can use our contact form to ask about or request deletion of a child’s information.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards — such as hashing passwords (never stored readable) and encrypting data in transit. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

We will give at least 30 days’ notice of material changes — by email to account holders and with a notice on the site (sooner if required by law or security). Material privacy changes will not be applied retroactively to previously collected data without your consent. If a change is significant, we may ask you to accept the new version the next time you sign in. Every version is dated above so you can see what changed.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: please use our contact form — it is the way to reach us, and we read every message. You can also find us on Instagram.
